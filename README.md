# Sakura River Valley

A real-time three.js scene: a boat poled down a misty river valley through a day of golden hour, night storm and dawn.

**Live:** https://dachidahu.github.io/valley/

The whole scene is the single self-contained `index.html` (needs a browser with WebGL 2). Controls are listed on the loading screen (press H in the scene to see them again).

Credits (see also inside `index.html`): three.js (MIT); textures and scans from Poly Haven (CC0); the boatman (with his straw hat and cape) and the monk are the models of the original scene, https://valley.mengto.here.now/.
